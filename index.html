ONLY - the subject can be placed only in these slots (e.g. MTH only P1-P3). NEVER - the subject is never placed in these slots (e.g. PHE never P1). RESERVE - these slots are kept for this subject only, so nothing else is scheduled there. Applies to: ALL, Junior, Middle, Senior, a year (9) or a class name. Day: MON, MON-FRI, TUE,THU.
